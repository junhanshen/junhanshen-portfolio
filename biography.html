Biography

Junhan Shen (b. 2000, China) is a New York-based visual artist whose practice spans painting, drawing, and printmaking.

She holds an MA in Art and Art Education from Columbia University and a BFA with Honors from the School of Visual Arts, with additional training at the China Academy of Art and the SVA Paris Program.

Starting at age 13, Shen moved between Huzhou, Brisbane, Melbourne, Chiang Rai, Paris, and now New York. Her work draws from remembered landscapes, poetry, literary texts, and personal experience, exploring perception and sensory experience through fluid color and an open-ended process.
