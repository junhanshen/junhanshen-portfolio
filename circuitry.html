Circuitry

Studioninedee, 508 West 26th Street, 9D, New York, NY
October 22 – 30, 2026
Opening reception: Thursday, October 22, 6–8 pm

A group exhibition curated by Caroline Heffron and presented by Artists Connected Projects, bringing artists and their invited colleagues together through shared themes, materials and inquiry. Through exchanges of color, medium and imagery, Circuitry explores memory, ecology, identity and transformation.

Participating artists

Yasmeen Abdallah, Natale Adgnot, Earth Ængel, Nicole Basilone, Sue Beyer, Julia Blume, Teja Clayman, Nicole Cooper, Nancy Elsamanoudi, Yining Fang, Shiraz Fazli, Caroline Heffron, Ivy Hickam, Sierra Iturrino, Nicole Licht, Emily McGill, Megan Nugroho, Avani Patel, Andrea Carola Robes, Christopher Santiago, Melinda Kiefer Santiago, Lisa Schonberg, Junhan Shen, Darcy Spitz, Christl Stringer, Sam Ticknor, Noelle Timmons, Tracy von Ahsen and Dayra Warner.
