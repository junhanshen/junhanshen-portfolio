Video description

Close-up sequences move across layers of red, turquoise and blue paint and textured marks on canvas. A hand works with a paintbrush. Text slides introduce impermanence, the literary sources for Untranslated Things and the artist’s approach to painting.

On-screen text

Impermanence describes the condition of things being temporary and continually subject to change. Bodies, thoughts, memories, relationships, identities, and knowledge all change through time, experience, and our encounters with the world and with others.

This project centers on the theme of impermanence through six texts from different cultural contexts and historical periods, including plays, novels, and scientific writing. The selected works come from England, Malaysia, Britain, Italy, and Germany, spanning from the early seventeenth century to the present.

When I paint, I imagine the canvas, and indeed the entire room, as a pool of water where thoughts and raw emotions flow, collide, and evolve, guiding me into a meditative state of creation.
